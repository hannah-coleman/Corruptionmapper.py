Case review

Enter the records for a case: reports, booking records, policies or SOPs, news or public statements, and a person's own recollection. The app lines them up and shows where they differ. It does not say which record is accurate or why they differ, treats recollections as given and not as established, and gives no legal advice. Private individuals are replaced with codes in reports unless they agreed to be named. Entries stay on this computer.
