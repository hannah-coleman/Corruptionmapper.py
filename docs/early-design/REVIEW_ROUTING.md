# Review routing protocol

The mapper is an analytic and routing aid. It does not determine guilt, decide whether a crime occurred, or replace counsel, an agency ombudsman, an inspector general, a court, or emergency services.

## Desk assignment

- **Casefile reviewer:** source quality, duplicate reporting, missing metadata, identity resolution, and graph accuracy.
- **Records custodian:** missing documents, version conflicts, retention questions, public-record requests, and procedural records.
- **Counsel:** privilege, discovery, subpoenas, preservation, appeals, retaliation, defamation, privacy, and publication decisions.
- **Qualified technical expert:** physics, biology, pathology, laboratory, environmental, or other specialized evidence.
- **Safety support:** immediate threats, child or disability safety, stalking, doxxing, or other urgent protection concerns. Use emergency services when there is imminent danger.
- **Oversight or complaint channel:** only after the issue is documented, jurisdiction is confirmed, and counsel or the applicable procedure identifies the correct destination.

## Handoff packet

Every routed flag should contain:

- one-sentence neutral issue statement
- exact source passages and URLs
- event chronology and relevant versions
- governing rule or baseline
- what is established, inferred, and unknown
- supporting, exculpatory, and contradictory material
- privacy, privilege, safety, and deadline warnings
- requested action and the person or desk responsible
- response, disposition, date, and escalation path

## Routing safeguards

- High severity means potential importance or urgency for review, not guilt.
- No automated filing, accusation, contact, or public release.
- A human must accept, re-route, defer, or close every flag.
- Closure requires a reason such as supported, contradicted, insufficient evidence, duplicate, outside scope, or counsel decision.
- Protect the casefile with least-privilege access, encrypted backups, immutable exports, and an audit log.
