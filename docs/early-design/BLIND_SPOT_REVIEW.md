# Blind-spot review

No investigation can guarantee that it found everything. This review reduces predictable omissions and makes uncertainty visible.

## Questions about the case

- What is the precise protected interest or harm being examined?
- What is the narrowest testable question?
- What facts would make the concern less likely or explain it innocently?
- Who could be harmed by an incorrect conclusion?
- Which claims are necessary for the legal objective, and which are merely interesting?

## Questions about records

- Which agency, clerk, custodian, contractor, insurer, auditor, or oversight body may hold a copy?
- What retention schedule, deletion policy, version history, or migration could explain a gap?
- Did the review include attachments, appendices, metadata, corrections, superseding orders, and appeals?
- Were records requested in the correct format and from the correct custodian?
- Is a missing record actually proof of absence, or only proof that this search did not find it?
- Are there public records in adjacent jurisdictions or state/federal systems?

## Questions about people and entities

- Could two names refer to different people or two names refer to one entity?
- Were roles and authority valid on the date of the event?
- Did an entity change names, owners, agents, addresses, or legal status?
- Is a relationship direct, inferred, temporary, professional, or merely coincidental?
- Were non-involved people unnecessarily included?

## Questions about time and process

- Are all dates in the same timezone and calendar convention?
- What did each actor know, and when could they reasonably have known it?
- Were deadlines, notices, quorum, voting, delegation, recusal, and approval rules satisfied?
- Was there an emergency exception, correction, clerical mistake, or later ratification?
- Does the apparent pattern survive comparison with similar ordinary cases?

## Questions about sources

- Is the source primary, derivative, anonymous, edited, summarized, or machine-generated?
- Can the original be accessed and preserved?
- Are multiple articles or posts repeating one underlying source?
- Could the source be spoofed, altered, truncated, out of context, or misdated?
- Is the quotation exact, and does the surrounding context change its meaning?
- Has the source been independently corroborated?

## Questions about social and video evidence

- Was the content publicly accessible without bypassing controls?
- Were platform terms, robots rules, API limits, and privacy expectations respected?
- Is the account authentic enough for the narrow proposition being made?
- Was the content edited, reposted, clipped, or stripped of metadata?
- Was the date, location, speaker, and chain of custody independently established?
- Are minors, medical details, home addresses, or vulnerable people exposed?

## Questions about scientific or medical claims

- Is there a measured observation, or only a pattern noticed by a person?
- Is the proposed mechanism testable and within the expert's field?
- Are there controls, baseline data, calibrated instruments, validated methods, and chain of custody?
- Could timing be correlation rather than causation?
- Has a qualified professional reviewed it, and are limitations recorded?

## Questions about legal and ethical exposure

- Could collection violate access restrictions, terms, privacy law, privilege, sealing rules, or a court order?
- Could publication create defamation, harassment, retaliation, or witness-safety risk?
- Is discovery, subpoena, preservation, or confidential material being handled only by counsel?
- Is the casefile access-controlled, backed up, encrypted, and logged?
- Is sensitive data minimized, redacted, and given a deletion schedule?
- Does the work accidentally reveal a protected address, disability information, child information, or medical record?

## Questions about the software

- Can every automated flag explain the exact source fields and rule that triggered it?
- Can a reviewer correct an entity merge, false positive, or source classification without destroying history?
- Are timestamps, hashes, imports, exports, and edits audit-logged?
- Are parser failures, partial downloads, robots denials, and unavailable pages visible?
- Are backups tested and exports readable without the application?
- Does the UI show confidence, limitations, counterevidence, and unresolved status prominently?
- Are there tests for duplicate sources, conflicting values, missing fields, timezone errors, and malformed imports?

## Exit criteria for a report

A report is not ready until the casefile has a defined scope, complete source register, preserved passages, governing rule, chronology, discrepancy analysis, independent corroboration attempt, exculpatory review, limitations, safety/privacy review, and counsel review where appropriate.
