# Karma AI extension chapters

These chapters are designed to be stand-alone tools or integratable modules. They share the same evidence model and can collaborate through the same case graph.

## 1. Core investigation chapter
Purpose: broad case intake and record management.
Includes:
- case creation
- entity creation
- source intake
- note and timeline tracking
- status workflow

## 2. Public records and FOIA chapter
Purpose: turn open questions into lawful requests and track responses.
Includes:
- request templates
- agency routing
- deadlines
- denial / exemption logs
- document upload and extraction
- correlation with case entities

## 3. Local government records chapter
Purpose: build the public-record map for city, county, and state sources.
Includes:
- agendas and minutes
- procurement files
- public notices
- budgets and audit packs
- transparent records and budget trails

## 4. Cold case chapter
Purpose: support old or unresolved matters with searchable relationships.
Includes:
- unresolved leads
- timeline reconstruction
- source comparison
- entity linking
- witness and record cross-reference

## 5. Law enforcement records chapter
Purpose: review public-facing records and patterns in agency behavior.
Includes:
- public complaints
- policy docs
- spending or contract patterns
- public-facing transparency records
- staffing and scheduling signals if lawfully obtained

## 6. Ethics and oversight chapter
Purpose: track campaign, ethics, and accountability signals.
Includes:
- public filings
- disclosures
- audit findings
- procurement irregularity checks
- public-interest compliance patterns

## 7. Anomaly detection chapter
Purpose: surface recurring signals across matters.
Includes:
- shared addresses
- repeated vendors
- payment/contract mismatch
- missing record trails
- unusual timing or relationship clustering

## 8. Person and entity review chapter
Purpose: map all public identities and relationships connected to a matter.
Includes:
- aliases and variants
- agency-role overlays
- relationship graphing
- cross-case linkage

## 9. Reporting and evidence export chapter
Purpose: prepare counsel-reviewed or publication-ready outputs.
Includes:
- timeline export
- evidence bundles
- redaction support
- narrative report builder

## 10. Collaboration chapter
Purpose: enable multiple user and AI roles to work on the same case.
Includes:
- reviewer notes
- comment threads
- evidence decision logs
- AI role assignment
- permissions and export controls

## Design principle for chapters

Each chapter should be able to:
- operate independently
- integrate into the shared graph
- be enabled or disabled by user choice
- preserve evidence provenance
- support human review before publication
