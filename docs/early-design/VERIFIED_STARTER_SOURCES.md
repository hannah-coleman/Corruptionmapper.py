# Verified starter source pack

Checked 2026-08-28 by resolving the page and reading its title where available. These are starting points, not findings. Review current terms and access rules before collecting.

## Local government and law enforcement

- City of Paragould portal: https://ar-paragould.civicplus.com/
  - Start with council, clerk, agendas, minutes, ordinances, budgets, procurement, permits, boards, and notices.
- Paragould Police Department: https://www.paragouldpolice.org/
  - Start with policies, public information, complaint process, records contact, and public releases.
- Greene County official site: https://www.greenecounty.arkansas.gov/
  - Start with Quorum Court, departments, clerk, budgets, bids, notices, and public records contact.

## Arkansas courts and business

- Arkansas CourtConnect: https://caseinfo.arcourts.gov/cconnect/
  - Case search, docket events, and permitted public documents. Distinguish filings from judicial findings.
- Arkansas Secretary of State: https://www.sos.arkansas.gov/
  - Business entity search, formation records, officers/agents, amendments, and status.

## Ethics, money, and oversight

- Arkansas Ethics Commission: https://www.arkansasethics.com/
  - Ethics, campaign finance, lobbying, and public disclosure information.
- Arkansas transparency portal: https://transparency.arkansas.gov/
  - State expenditures, revenues, employee compensation, and related transparency links.
- Arkansas Legislative Audit: https://www.arklegaudit.gov/
  - Audit reports, findings, and oversight material.
- Arkansas Attorney General: https://arkansasag.gov/
  - Official opinions, consumer/public-interest information, and agency contacts where relevant.

## Federal sources, only when jurisdiction fits

- FBI public tips: https://tips.fbi.gov/
  - Use only for matters within federal jurisdiction and after counsel review; do not submit unsupported allegations.
- USAspending: https://www.usaspending.gov/
  - Federal awards and spending.
- SEC EDGAR: https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/
  - Public filings for entities required to file with the SEC; not a general LLC database.

## Records-request destinations

- City and county records: use the official clerk or records-custodian contact identified on the official portal. Request specific records, dates, and formats.
- Arkansas state records: use the relevant agency's official records process.
- Arkansas courts: use the clerk or court-established process, not federal FOIA.
- Federal agencies: use the specific agency FOIA office or official FOIA portal.
- Medical, CPS, child, school, sealed, privileged, or protected material: do not bulk collect; ask counsel about authorized process.
