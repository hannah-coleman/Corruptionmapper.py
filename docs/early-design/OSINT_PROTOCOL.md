# Verified OSINT protocol

This workspace is for lawful public-interest research and defense documentation. It is not a tool for harassment, intimidation, doxxing, or publishing accusations without support.

## Evidentiary standard

Every person and organization remains presumed innocent unless a competent authority or legally tested evidence establishes otherwise. The workspace records observable conduct, public documents, procedural comparisons, and competing explanations; it does not determine guilt. A relationship, complaint, delay, error, omission, or policy violation may justify further review but is not, by itself, proof of corruption or criminal intent.

For each material issue, preserve both supporting and exculpatory information, identify the applicable rule or policy, state what the evidence actually establishes, and mark what remains unknown. Never omit a source because it weakens the working hypothesis.

## Collection boundary

- Use public records, official databases, public court filings, public meeting materials, and publicly viewable social content.
- Follow each platform's terms, robots rules, rate limits, and available official export or API options.
- Do not bypass access controls, use fake identities, join private groups under false pretenses, buy breached data, guess passwords, or collect sensitive personal data that is not necessary to the defined question.
- Do not contact, pressure, impersonate, or surveil a person as part of collection. Route legal demands and preservation issues through counsel.
- Do not collect, download, store, or redistribute leaked or improperly disclosed child-welfare, medical, disability, school, or other protected records. Record only minimal routing metadata and follow counsel's instructions.

## Evidence record

For each item, record:

- source title, issuing body or account, original URL, and record type
- publication/post date and the date/time captured, including timezone
- exact quotation, page/paragraph or video timestamp, and what the item actually proves
- local filename, file hash where possible, and whether it is an original, copy, screenshot, or transcription
- collection method and any access limitations
- linked entities, confidence, and contradictions

A complaint, post, or video is an allegation or lead unless independently corroborated. Preserve the original wording and label interpretation separately.

## Audit sequence

1. Define one testable question.
2. Set jurisdiction, agencies, record types, and date range.
3. Collect primary sources before commentary.
4. Preserve public social evidence without altering it.
5. Separate sourced facts, inferences, and unresolved leads.
6. Request narrowly described missing public records from the custodian.
7. Corroborate material claims with an independent source.
8. Have counsel review privacy, privilege, sealed records, defamation, retaliation, and publication risk.

## Working URCHIN / STARR framework

This is the current working interpretation supplied by the case researcher. Definitions and weighting should be confirmed before it drives automated prioritization.

**U.R.C.H.I.N. — Universal Refraction of Consilient Hierarchical Interference Nexus**

- **Universal:** collect across public agencies, courts, procurement, business filings, meeting records, journalism, and public social sources while applying the same provenance standard.
- **Refraction:** examine how an event changes as it passes through institutions, records, witnesses, agencies, and public reporting; preserve the original wording and identify where interpretation or omission appears.
- **Consilient:** look for independent sources that converge on the same fact; do not count duplicated reporting as independent corroboration.
- **Hierarchical:** distinguish individual, organization, contractor, agency, and oversight levels without assuming that rank proves responsibility.
- **Interference:** log observable delays, missing attachments, changed records, redactions, contradictory statements, or procedural departures as events requiring explanation, not proof of intent.
- **Nexus:** map the specific, sourced connection between nodes and record whether it is direct, inferred, or unresolved.

**S.T.A.R.R. — Spatiotemporal Threat Assessment & Risk Response**

- **Spatiotemporal:** compare locations, dates, sequence, custody, and decision points.
- **Threat assessment:** document concrete safety or retaliation indicators, preserving exact words and context; do not label ordinary disagreement as a threat.
- **Risk:** prioritize public-interest, evidentiary, legal, privacy, and personal-safety risks separately.
- **Response:** track what each institution or person did after an event, including notices, investigations, denials, delays, corrective actions, escalation, or silence; distinguish documented response from inferred motive.

The application should use this framework to create review questions and discrepancy flags, never to infer criminality or identify a person as corrupt. Keep an assumptions log for every interpretation until the researcher confirms the intended meanings.

## Immediate safety

If there is an immediate threat to you or your son, prioritize emergency services and your lawyer's safety plan. Keep an incident log with dates, times, witnesses, documents, and exact words; avoid direct confrontation or public accusations while facts are being verified.
